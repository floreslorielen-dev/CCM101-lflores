# Mission Reflection

Completing Mission 3 allowed me to explore the three leading cloud platforms—Amazon Web Services (AWS), Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform (GCP)—and understand how each one addresses different business needs. Among them, I found Google Cloud Platform the most impressive because of its strong focus on Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Kubernetes. Its developer-friendly console and innovative services such as Vertex AI and Tensor Processing Units highlight Google’s strength in modern computing. This made me realize how powerful GCP can be for startups and research companies that rely on advanced data solutions.

At the same time, I noticed that AWS, Azure, and GCP share many similarities. All three provide compute services through virtual machines, storage solutions for data, networking features to connect resources, and identity management for security. They also maintain global infrastructure with regions and availability zones, ensuring reliability and scalability. These similarities show that while each platform has unique strengths, they all aim to deliver secure and flexible cloud services.

The most important lesson I learned is that business requirements drive cloud platform selection. A startup may choose GCP for cost-effectiveness, a university may prefer Azure for seamless Microsoft integration, and a global e-commerce company may rely on AWS for its broad service range and worldwide infrastructure. Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant helped me think critically about aligning technology with client needs. My GitHub portfolio now reflects this growth, with organized documentation, comparison tables, and recommendations that demonstrate my learning journey in cloud computing.
